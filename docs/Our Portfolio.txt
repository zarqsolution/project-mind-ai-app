FreightAnywhere is a full-cycle, web-based TMS we built for freight brokerages and carriers, covering the entire life of a load in one platform: quoting, order entry, carrier sourcing, dispatch, live GPS tracking, billing, carrier settlement, and invoice auditing.


Key modules: Shipment board with multi-filter search, saved views, and one-click actions (quotes, orders, load board posting, invoicing, dispatch reports). Order workspace with a visual lifecycle pipeline, built-in customer credit control, and a Rate Maker with Carrier Target Pay and Max Buy Rate for margin protection. Carrier management and a Pallet Rate System. Live tracking on Trimble Maps with color-coded statuses (Delivered, In-Transit, Covered, Late, Missing) and automated tracking logs. A complete accounting back office: AR aging, customer statements, carrier settlements with deduction management, balance sheet, income statement, commission reports, and factoring support. An email-native invoice auditing inbox with team assignment and status workflow, extended by an AI-assisted auditing layer (Tech-AI Auditing, Beta).


What sets it apart: one record from quote to cash with no re-keying, credit enforcement at the point of booking, margin guardrails baked into rating, real-time visibility, and AI-powered audit, replacing the usual stack of a TMS, tracking tool, accounting software, and email folders with a single application.


Special Features & Integrations


- DAT Load Board integration – post loads to DAT directly from the shipment board, with Posted and Reserved status badges synced back to the order grid. No re-keying between the TMS and the load board.
- Trimble Maps integration – commercial truck routing, live GPS position mapping, and mileage calculation (Run Miles) built into tracking and rating.
- EAL connectivity – one-click "Send Load to EAL" from the order screen for instant carrier sourcing.
- Email-native invoice auditing – carrier invoices flow by email straight into an audit inbox with team assignment, To Do / In Progress / In Review / Done statuses, and full reply/forward without leaving the platform.
- Tech-AI Auditing (Beta) – AI-assisted verification of invoices and accounting data layered on top of the manual audit workflow.
- Credit enforcement at booking – customer credit limit, used, and available balance shown on the order itself, blocking over-extension before it happens.
- Margin guardrails – Carrier Target Pay and Max Buy Rate caps in the Rate Maker protect margin on every load.
- Full back-office accounting – AR aging, carrier settlements with deductions, balance sheet, income statement, commissions, and a dedicated factoring companies module, all inside the TMS.
- API Settings module – open integration layer for connecting external systems, load boards, and tracking providers.
- Automated tracking logs – GPS check-ins captured automatically with Late Tracking and Missing Tracking alerts.
